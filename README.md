# SG Mall Parking Rates

Search and compare car park charges at Singapore shopping centres by area and day (weekday, Saturday, Sunday & PH).

**Live site:** https://nwm1997-blip.github.io/sg-mall-parking/

Data: [Carpark Rates](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_9f6056bdb6b1dfba57f063593e4f34ae/view), Land Transport Authority, via data.gov.sg (survey dated Nov 2018). Rates may have changed since; confirm with the mall before you go. First-hour figures are estimates parsed from the rate text.
